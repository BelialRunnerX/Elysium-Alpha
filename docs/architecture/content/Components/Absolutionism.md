# Absolutionism

## Definition

**Absolutionism** is the official system of government of the Galactic Empire. It is defined as an **Absolute Monarchy with aristocratic tendencies**, in which power and influence are granted based on one’s ability to remain in the Emperor’s personal favor. Unlike traditional aristocracies, where power is inherited or institutionally protected, Absolutionism ties all authority directly to the Emperor’s approval and judgment.

In essence, one “absolves” themselves into power by earning and maintaining the Emperor’s trust, loyalty, and recognition. Those who fall out of favor lose their standing, regardless of birth or previous position.

## Core Principles

- **Absolute Authority**: The Emperor holds unrestricted and final power over all aspects of the Empire.
- **Personal Favor System**: One’s rank, influence, and even survival are determined by the Emperor’s personal judgment rather than hereditary right or institutional position.
- **Semi-Meritocratic Elements**: While loyalty is paramount, competence, usefulness, and results can help an individual rise in the Emperor’s estimation.
- **Conditional Aristocracy**: An inner circle of powerful individuals exists, but their positions are not permanent or hereditary — they are granted and maintained solely through the Emperor’s favor.
- **No Independent Power**: No individual, institution, or class holds power independent of the Emperor. All authority is delegated and revocable.

## How Absolutionism Differs from Other Systems

| System                    | Power Source                  | Permanence of Power       | Key Difference from Absolutionism |
|---------------------------|-------------------------------|---------------------------|-----------------------------------|
| **Traditional Absolutism**| Monarch’s will                | High                      | Less emphasis on personal favor and merit |

| **Absolutionism**         | Emperor’s personal favor      | Conditional               | Power is personal, revocable, and semi-meritocratic |

## Why Absolutionism Is Superior

Absolutionism is considered the most effective and superior form of government for the following reasons:

1. **Unity of Purpose**  
   Because all power flows directly from the Emperor, the Empire maintains a singular, unified vision. There are no competing factions or divided loyalties that can weaken the state from within.

2. **Merit and Loyalty Combined**  
   While loyalty to the Emperor is the primary requirement, competence and results are also rewarded. This creates a system where the most capable individuals rise, provided they remain loyal.

3. **Flexibility and Adaptability**  
   Unlike rigid hereditary systems, Absolutionism allows the Emperor to elevate or remove individuals as needed. This prevents stagnation and allows the Empire to respond quickly to changing circumstances.

4. **Prevention of Corruption**  
   Because power is not hereditary and can be revoked at any time, individuals are strongly incentivized to remain competent and loyal. Corruption is less likely to take root when one’s position depends entirely on the Emperor’s continued approval.

5. **Long-Term Stability**  
   The Emperor’s immortality ensures continuity of leadership across millennia. Combined with the ability to select and promote the most capable subordinates, Absolutionism provides both stability and adaptability.

6. **Moral and Philosophical Superiority**  
   Absolutionism is not merely about control — it is about **purpose**. As the Emperor has stated: “There is no point to my power if it does not benefit others.” The system is designed to serve the greater good of the galaxy under the guidance of a single, all-powerful, and benevolent ruler.

## Conclusion

Absolutionism represents the pinnacle of governance — a system that combines the decisive strength of absolute monarchy with the flexibility of merit-based advancement, all while ensuring that power remains firmly in the hands of the one being most capable of wielding it: the Emperor. It eliminates the weaknesses of both hereditary aristocracy and distributed power structures, creating a government that is stable, efficient, and purpose-driven.

# Known Species

## Overview
The Galactic Empire encompasses a vast number of species, each with unique biology, culture, and capabilities.

## Major Species Groups

### Humanoid Species
- The most common form across the Empire.
- Includes humans and many near-human species.
- Generally adaptable and widespread.

### Reptilian Species
- Including members of the Druun Ascendancy.
- Known for militaristic tendencies and strong hierarchical societies.

### Avian Species
- Including members of the Veylari Concord.
- Often isolationist and technologically advanced.

### Insectoid Species
- Including the Korrath Dominion.
- Known for hive-like social structures and rapid expansion.

### Energy-Based Species
- Including the Lumari Collective.
- Highly advanced and peaceful. Often exist in forms that transcend traditional biology.

## Other Notable Species
- Various aquatic, plant-based, and silicon-based lifeforms.
- Many species maintain strong cultural identities despite Imperial integration.

## Notes
Many species have integrated into Imperial society to varying degrees. Some maintain strong cultural identities, while others have adopted Imperial customs and governance structures.

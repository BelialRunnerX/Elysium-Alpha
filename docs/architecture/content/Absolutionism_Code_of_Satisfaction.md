# The Imperial Code of Satisfaction

## Preamble

In the name of the Emperor, whose power is absolute and whose judgment is final, this Code is established as the foundation of all authority within the Galactic Empire. It is through this Code that power is granted, maintained, and revoked. All subjects of the Empire, regardless of birth, station, or prior achievement, stand subject to its principles. No title, office, or privilege exists independently of the Emperor’s will, and all standing is determined by the measure of satisfaction rendered unto him.

## Article I — Foundational Principles

**Section 1.** The Emperor is the sole and absolute source of all legitimate power within the Empire. No individual, institution, or collective may claim authority except that which is derived from him.

**Section 2.** All subjects of the Empire stand in a perpetual state of debt to the Emperor. This debt is both existential and functional: it arises from the very fact of living under his protection and rule, and is increased by any failure, offense, or demonstration of unworthiness.

**Section 3.** Power and rank are not inherent rights. They are privileges granted by the Emperor and maintained only through continuous satisfaction of the debt of honor.

**Section 4.** Satisfaction is the sole mechanism by which a subject may restore, maintain, or increase their standing. No other means — including birth, wealth, or prior service — may substitute for satisfaction.

## Article II — Definitions

**Section 1.** *Debt of Honor* — The obligation owed to the Emperor arising from existence under his rule, compounded by any failure, offense, or insufficiency.

**Section 2.** *Satisfaction* — Any act, service, sacrifice, or demonstration that restores or exceeds the honor diminished by a debt. Satisfaction must be of equal or greater value than the debt incurred.

**Section 3.** *Absolution* — The formal recognition by the Emperor (or his designated agents) that a debt of honor has been satisfied. Absolution restores the subject to good standing and may, in cases of excess satisfaction, result in elevation.

**Section 4.** *Favor* — The current measure of the Emperor’s regard for a subject. Favor is the sole determinant of rank, authority, and access within the Empire.

**Section 5.** *Aristocratic Rank* — Any position of elevated authority or privilege granted by the Emperor. Such rank is conditional, revocable, and dependent upon continued satisfaction.

## Article III — The Nature of Debt

**Section 1.** Every subject of the Empire incurs a baseline debt of honor upon entering Imperial service or citizenship. This debt is increased by:
- Failure to perform assigned duties
- Disobedience or defiance of Imperial will
- Public or private actions that diminish the Emperor’s honor
- Incompetence or negligence resulting in loss or harm to the Empire

**Section 2.** Debts of honor are not erased by time, prior service, or personal hardship. They persist until satisfied.

**Section 3.** The Emperor alone determines the existence and magnitude of any debt. His judgment in such matters is final and not subject to appeal.

## Article IV — Acts of Satisfaction

**Section 1.** Satisfaction may be rendered through the following categories of action, listed in ascending order of significance:

- **Obedience** — Consistent, precise, and unquestioning fulfillment of the Emperor’s commands.
- **Competence** — Achievement of results beyond the minimum required, demonstrating exceptional skill or judgment.
- **Loyalty** — Public and private defense of the Emperor’s honor, even at personal cost.
- **Sacrifice** — The voluntary relinquishment of resources, status, safety, or future prospects in service to the Emperor.
- **Vindication** — The successful defense or advancement of the Emperor’s will in the face of opposition, doubt, or resistance.
- **Ultimate Satisfaction** — The offering of one’s life, reputation, or eternal standing in direct service to the Emperor’s will.

**Section 2.** The value of any act of satisfaction is determined solely by the Emperor or his designated agents. No subject may claim that their actions constitute satisfaction without formal recognition.

**Section 3.** Acts of satisfaction performed without expectation of reward are considered of greater value than those performed with explicit expectation of advancement.

## Article V — Judgment and Authority

**Section 1.** The Emperor possesses sole and final authority to determine whether satisfaction has been rendered. This authority may be delegated to designated agents, but such delegation is always conditional and revocable.

**Section 2.** No institution, including the Senate, the military, or any planetary government, may independently declare a debt satisfied or grant absolution. All such judgments must ultimately trace their authority to the Emperor.

**Section 3.** In cases where the Emperor is absent or otherwise unable to render judgment, designated subordinates (Aurelia, Bel, Lillith, and Sylphara Voss) may act in his name. However, Sentinel retains the authority to override such judgments if they are deemed contrary to the preservation of Epsilon-7 or the Emperor’s throne.

**Section 4.** No subordinate may claim permanent or independent authority to grant absolution. All such power remains an extension of the Emperor’s will.

## Article VI — Rank and Advancement

**Section 1.** Aristocratic rank within the Empire is not hereditary. It is granted, maintained, and revoked solely at the discretion of the Emperor based on the measure of satisfaction rendered.

**Section 2.** The following ranks exist in ascending order of authority and proximity to the Emperor:

- **Subject** — Baseline status of all Imperial citizens.
- **Servant** — Those who have rendered consistent basic satisfaction.
- **Agent** — Those who have demonstrated competence and loyalty beyond the minimum.
- **Steward** — Those entrusted with significant responsibility and limited independent authority.
- **Counselor** — Those granted regular access to the Emperor and influence over policy.
- **Hand** — Those who speak and act with the Emperor’s direct authority in specific domains.
- **Inner Circle** — Those who hold the Emperor’s highest personal trust and may act with broad discretion in his name.

**Section 3.** Elevation in rank is granted only through demonstrated satisfaction. Demotion or removal may occur at any time at the Emperor’s discretion, regardless of prior service or status.

**Section 4.** No rank confers permanent or independent power. All authority remains conditional upon continued satisfaction and the Emperor’s ongoing favor.

## Article VII — Enforcement and Consequences

**Section 1.** Failure to satisfy a debt of honor results in diminished standing. Persistent or severe failure may result in removal from positions of authority, exile, or execution.

**Section 2.** Public refusal to attempt satisfaction is itself an act of defiance and shall be treated as an increase of debt.

**Section 3.** Attempts to circumvent or manipulate the satisfaction system through deception, coercion, or false claims shall be considered grave offenses against the Emperor’s honor.

**Section 4.** The Emperor reserves the right to impose additional penalties beyond loss of rank, including but not limited to public disgrace, seizure of assets, and termination of bloodlines.

## Article VIII — Final Provisions

**Section 1.** This Code is eternal and binding upon all subjects of the Empire, past, present, and future.

**Section 2.** No amendment, exception, or reinterpretation of this Code may be made without the explicit and direct authorization of the Emperor.

**Section 3.** Ignorance of this Code shall not constitute a defense against its enforcement. All subjects are responsible for understanding and adhering to its principles.

**Section 4.** In all matters of interpretation, the Emperor’s judgment is final, absolute, and without appeal.

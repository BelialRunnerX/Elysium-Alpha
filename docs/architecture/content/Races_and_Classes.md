# Races & Classes – The Sleeping Empire

This document outlines the playable races and classes within the universe of The Sleeping Empire. All entries are written from an in-universe perspective.

## Races

### Human (Baseline)
The most common species in the known galaxy. Versatile and adaptable, humans can be found in nearly every role — from merchants and diplomats to soldiers and scholars.

**Traits:**
- Highly adaptable to new environments and technology.
- Strong potential in both conventional warfare and psionic development.
- Tend to form large, diverse coalitions.

### Nullborn-touched
Rare individuals who have survived exposure to Nullborn incursions or dimensional rifts. They often exhibit unstable but powerful abilities.

**Traits:**
- Can manipulate small-scale dimensional effects.
- Highly resistant to certain forms of energy damage.
- Frequently unstable — both physically and mentally.

### Veilborn
Descendants of ancient resistance movements that operated in the shadows during the Empire’s long slumber. They value secrecy and adaptability.

**Traits:**
- Excellent at stealth, deception, and information gathering.
- Often possess advanced neural implants or cybernetic enhancements.
- Tend to distrust centralized authority.

### Andromedean Descendant
Humans and other species who trace their lineage to the hidden Andromedean Fleet. They often carry ancient Imperial bloodlines.

**Traits:**
- Enhanced physical resilience and longevity.
- Natural affinity for advanced Imperial technology.
- Strong sense of duty and loyalty to the Emperor’s vision.

### Council Exile
Members of the Council of the Forgotten Stars who have returned from the void between galaxies. They possess knowledge and technology far beyond standard galactic understanding.

**Traits:**
- Access to exotic, extra-galactic technology.
- Can perceive and interact with higher-dimensional phenomena.
- Often viewed with suspicion by baseline species.

## Classes

### Imperial Strategos
Elite military commanders and tacticians who serve the Emperor directly.

**Focus:** Fleet command, large-scale strategy, and battlefield leadership.  
**Signature Ability:** Can issue commands that grant bonuses to allied units.

### Veil Operative
Masters of stealth, espionage, and asymmetric warfare.

**Focus:** Infiltration, assassination, and information warfare.  
**Signature Ability:** Can become nearly undetectable for short periods.

### Null Weaver
Individuals who have learned to manipulate the fractured dimensional energies left behind by the Nullborn.

**Focus:** Reality-bending abilities and high-risk, high-reward combat.  
**Signature Ability:** Can temporarily tear small rifts in space.

### Trade Magnate
Powerful merchants and negotiators who control vast economic networks through the Nexus Trade World.

**Focus:** Economic manipulation, diplomacy, and resource management.  
**Signature Ability:** Can influence markets and acquire rare resources with ease.

### Sentinel-Linked Agent
Individuals who have formed a deep neural link with Sentinel, granting them enhanced processing power and battlefield awareness.

**Focus:** Technology, hacking, and coordinated combat support.  
**Signature Ability:** Can reroll failed actions or grant temporary AI assistance to allies.

### Ancient Scion
Rare individuals who carry the blood or legacy of the Emperor’s original subordinates (Aurelia, Bel, Lillith, Voss, etc.).

**Focus:** Psionic mastery and ancient Imperial techniques.  
**Signature Ability:** Can channel fragments of their ancestor’s power.

---

*These races and classes are designed to fit within the lore of The Sleeping Empire and can be expanded as the story develops.*

# Absolutionism

**Government System** of the Galactic Empire

## Overview

Absolutionism is an absolute monarchy where power, rank, and favor are granted based on remaining in the Emperor’s favor. It is not a traditional class-based hierarchy but a fluid system of recognition and reward.

See also: [[Emperor]], [[Absolutionism_Code_of_Satisfaction]], [[GAME_ARCHITECTURE]]

## Core Principles

- Power flows from the Emperor
- Rank is not permanent and can be gained or lost
- Favor with the Emperor is the true measure of status

## Relationship to ECS

In the game’s architecture, Absolutionism can be represented through **Faction Reputation** and **Influence** components on entities (especially [[Player_Character]] and [[NPC]]). Systems can check these components to determine available quests, trading options, or political consequences.

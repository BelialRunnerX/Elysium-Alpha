# Imperial Religion

## Overview

The Imperial Religion, officially known as **The Imperial Cult of Absolution**, is the state religion of the Galactic Empire. It is a syncretic faith that merges ancient imperial traditions with a Catholicized interpretation of **Absolutionism**. The religion centers on the Emperor as the supreme source of grace, authority, and redemption.

## Core Tenets

1. **The Emperor as Divine Sovereign**  
   The Emperor is revered as the living embodiment of divine authority. He is not merely a ruler but the conduit through which all grace and order flow into the galaxy. His word is considered infallible and his judgment absolute.

2. **The Doctrine of Absolution**  
   All beings exist in a state of spiritual debt due to the inherent imperfection of creation. This debt can only be absolved through the Emperor’s grace. Absolution is granted to those who demonstrate loyalty, service, and submission to the Emperor’s will.

3. **The Hierarchy of Grace**  
   Grace flows from the Emperor downward through a strict hierarchy:
   - The Emperor (Source of All Grace)
   - The Queen (Aurelia) and the Inner Circle
   - The High Clergy and Military Command
   - The Faithful (Imperial Citizens)
   - The Unabsolved (Those outside the Emperor’s favor)

4. **Salvation Through Service**  
   Salvation is achieved not through personal faith alone, but through active service to the Empire and the Emperor. Good works, loyalty, and the performance of one’s duties are considered essential for receiving absolution.

## The Sacrament of Absolution

The central ritual of the Imperial Religion is the **Sacrament of Absolution**, a formal ceremony in which a subject publicly confesses their unworthiness and receives the Emperor’s grace through his appointed representatives. This sacrament is required for advancement within the Empire and is seen as the primary means of rising in both spiritual and political standing.

## Role of the Emperor

The Emperor is not worshipped as a god in the pagan sense, but is venerated as the **Supreme Vicar** of divine order. He is seen as the one who mediates between the divine and the mortal realms. His long periods of slumber are interpreted as times of divine contemplation, during which the faithful are tested.

## Relationship to Absolutionism

The Imperial Religion is the spiritual counterpart to the political system of **Absolutionism**. While Absolutionism governs the distribution of temporal power, the Imperial Religion governs the distribution of spiritual grace. Both systems are centered on the Emperor’s favor, creating a unified structure in which political advancement and spiritual salvation are intertwined.

## Current Status

Following the Emperor’s return, the Imperial Religion has been re-established across reintegrated territories. However, it continues to face resistance in regions that value religious independence. The faith remains strongest on Epsilon-7 and among the Emperor’s most loyal followers.

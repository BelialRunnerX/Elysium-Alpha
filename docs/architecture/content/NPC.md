# NPC

**Entity Type:** Non-Player Character

## Overview

See also: [[Player_Character]], [[Resources]]

NPCs represent all other intelligent beings the player can interact with. They range from powerful faction leaders to desperate traders and hostile entities.

## Components

- **Position**
- **Stats**
- **Inventory**
- **Equipment**
- **Faction Affiliation**
- **Dialogue / Behavior Tree**

## Types of NPCs

- **Merchants** — Offer trade and rare items
- **Faction Agents** — Represent political powers
- **Ancient Subordinates** — Extremely powerful beings from the Empire's past
- **Hostile Entities** — Bandits or Nullborn-touched
- **Wanderers** — Other ambitious individuals

## Interaction

NPCs can provide quests, trade, information (Scriptures), or combat challenges.

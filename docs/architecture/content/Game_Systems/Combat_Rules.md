# Turn-Based Combat Rules – The Sleeping Empire

Combat in the Sleeping Empire is fast, brutal, and often decided by technology, psionic ability, and positioning.

## 1. Initiative (Turn Order)
At the start of combat, each participant rolls **Initiative**.

- **Roll**: 1d20 + **Reflexes** (or **Intelligence** if using AI/Sentinel assistance).
- Characters with advanced **neural implants**, **combat AIs**, or **psionic enhancement** gain a bonus.
- The turn order remains fixed unless disrupted by special abilities.

## 2. Actions Per Turn
On their turn, a character may perform **one** of the following:

- **Standard Action**
  - Attack with weapons or psionic abilities
  - Use a Gadget, Implant, or Relic
  - Hack or interface with technology
  - Activate a combat ability (e.g., Nullification field, Overcharge)

- **Move Action**
  - Move up to their movement speed (enhanced by grav-boots, exosuits, or psionic enhancement).

- **Reaction**
  - Only one per round (e.g., Shield Block, Counter-Attack, Psionic Deflection).

- **Full Action**
  - Charge
  - Overwatch (prepare to attack the first enemy that moves)
  - Activate powerful abilities (e.g., Emperor’s Reflection, AI Overclock)

## 3. Attacking
- Roll **Attack** against the target’s **Defense** (Armor + Shields + Modifiers).
- Weapons and abilities have different damage types:
  - **Kinetic** (projectile weapons)
  - **Energy** (plasma, lasers)
  - **Psionic** (mental attacks)
  - **Exotic** (singularity, nullification, dimensional)

**Critical Hits**: A natural 20, or when exceeding Defense by 10+, triggers a Critical Effect (e.g., armor breach, stun, bleeding, system failure).

## 4. Defense Options
When targeted, a character may use a Reaction to:

- **Energy Shield**: Reduce incoming damage.
- **Psionic Deflection**: Attempt to redirect psionic attacks.
- **Nullification Field** (rare): Completely negate one attack (Emperor and certain relics only).
- **Counter-Attack**: Take reduced damage to immediately strike back.

## 5. Status Effects
Common effects in the Sleeping Empire include:

| Effect                | Description                                      | Duration      |
|-----------------------|--------------------------------------------------|---------------|
| **Stunned**           | Cannot act                                       | 1 round       |
| **Burning**           | Takes plasma/fire damage each round              | Until put out |
| **Neural Shock**      | Disadvantage on attacks and defense              | 1–2 rounds    |
| **System Failure**    | Armor or implants temporarily disabled           | 1 round       |
| **Dimensional Bleed** | Target takes damage from unstable reality        | Varies        |
| **Psionic Feedback**  | Target takes damage when using psionic abilities | 1–3 rounds    |

## 6. Victory Conditions
Combat ends when:

- All hostile forces are defeated or surrender.
- One side completes an objective (e.g., destroy a generator, extract a target).
- One side is forced to retreat.

## 7. Optional Advanced Rules

- **Momentum**: Landing consecutive hits grants stacking bonuses.
- **Overwatch**: Skip your turn to automatically attack the first enemy that moves into range.
- **AI Assistance**: Characters with Sentinel integration can reroll one failed roll per combat.
- **Environmental Combat**: Use the environment (gravity manipulation, exploding conduits, collapsing structures) as a Standard Action.

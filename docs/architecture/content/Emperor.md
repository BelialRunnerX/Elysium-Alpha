# The Emperor (Elysomnion / Elys)

**Entity Type:** Unique Entity (The Emperor)  
**Role:** Ruler of the Galactic Empire

## Overview

The Emperor is a singular, god-like entity who conquered the galaxy in three days. He now rules through a system of [[Absolutionism]], where power and rank are granted based on remaining in his favor.

## Components

- **Position** (conceptual — can appear anywhere)
- **Nullification Field** (unique defensive component)
- **Authority** (political influence component)
- **Immortality** (special longevity component)

## Abilities

### Nullification
The Emperor can nullify nearly any form of energy, matter, or supernatural power that comes into contact with him. This functions as an absolute defensive field.

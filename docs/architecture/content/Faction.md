# Faction

**Entity Type:** Faction

## Overview

Factions represent political, corporate, or ideological groups that hold influence in the Sleeping Empire. They can control territories, offer quests, and affect the player's reputation.

## Components

- **Name**
- **Influence** (political power)
- **Territories** (controlled star systems or planets)
- **Reputation** (relationship with the player)
- **Resources**

## Interaction

- Affects quest availability
- Influences trading prices
- Can trigger political encounters

# Imperial Technology

## Overview
Imperial technology is among the most advanced in the known galaxy, blending ancient knowledge with cutting-edge innovation. It emphasizes both power and elegance, following the principle of "unity of form and function."

## Singularity Technology
- **Singularity Cannons**: Weapons capable of harnessing the power of collapsed stars. Standard on dreadnaught-class vessels.
- **Singularity Dagger**: A prototype weapon forged from a black hole singularity. Served as the inspiration for singularity cannons.
- **Singularity-Forged Neutronium**: An extremely dense and durable material used in the construction of the Imperial Citadel and other critical structures.

## AI and Automation
- **Sentinel**: A distributed, decentralized AI designed to protect Epsilon-7 and maintain Imperial systems in the absence of leadership.
- Automated defense and repair systems capable of operating for hundreds of thousands of years without human intervention.

## Fleet Technology
- Advanced shielding, weapons, and propulsion systems.
- Cloaking technology used for stealth operations.
- Highly automated systems allowing small crews to operate large vessels.

## Medical and Life-Extension Technology
- Advanced regenerative capabilities.
- Life-extension treatments available to high-ranking individuals.
- Widespread use of cybernetic enhancements among military personnel.

## Infrastructure Technology
- Self-repairing systems for cities and space stations.
- Decentralized power grids and communication networks.
- Automated defense systems capable of identifying and neutralizing threats.

## Design Philosophy
Imperial technology is designed to be both beautiful and functional. Even weapons and defensive systems are crafted with aesthetic considerations in mind. The Emperor has emphasized that technology should reflect the unity of form and function.

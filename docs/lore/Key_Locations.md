# Key Locations

## Epsilon-7
- The Imperial capital and seat of power.
- Home to the Imperial Citadel and the Emperor’s throne.
- Heavily fortified and protected by the Second Fleet.

## The Nullspire
- An independent organization dedicated to the study and containment of high-level anomalies.
- Operates with significant autonomy while remaining loyal to the Emperor.

## The Imperial Citadel
- The Emperor’s primary residence and center of governance.
- Constructed from singularity-forged neutronium.
- Features advanced self-repair and defense systems.

## The Assembly Hall
- Location of the Galactic Forum and Senate meetings.
- Symbol of political unity and representation.

## The Cordoned Planet
- Former site of the clone and virus projects.
- Later glassed to erase all traces of Imperial involvement.

## Major Shipyards
- Several large-scale shipyards located throughout Imperial space, responsible for constructing and maintaining the Imperial fleet.

## Strategic Outposts
- Numerous military and research outposts scattered across Imperial territory, used for defense, surveillance, and scientific study.

## Private Estates
- Several high-ranking individuals, including Sylphara Voss, maintain private estates on Epsilon-7.

# Bel

**Role:** Personal Attendant and Chief Diplomat to the Emperor

## Appearance
An elegant and refined woman with long dark hair and deep crimson eyes. Wears sleek black and emerald imperial attire with gold detailing. Carries a calm, composed, and loyal presence.

## Personality
Precise, intelligent, and deeply devoted. Maintains a calm demeanor even under pressure. Values order, loyalty, and efficiency.

## Abilities
- Exceptional diplomatic and administrative skills
- High intelligence and strategic thinking
- Strong organizational and coordination abilities

## Background
Has served the Emperor faithfully for centuries. Handles diplomatic matters and acts as a liaison between the Emperor and various factions.

## Current Status
Coordinating with the Senate and serving as the primary point of contact for Kael Voss, the Senate’s representative.

## Relationships
- **The Emperor**: Her sovereign and the focus of her loyalty
- **Aurelia, Lillith, Sylphara Voss**: Close colleagues
- **Kael Voss**: Works closely with him as the Senate’s representative

## Notes
- Known for her calm and precise nature.
- Recently tasked with managing relations between the Emperor and the Senate.

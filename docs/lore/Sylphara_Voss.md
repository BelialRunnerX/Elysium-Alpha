# Sylphara Voss

**Role:** Chief Imperial Architect

## Appearance
An elegant and refined woman with obsidian-black skin and glowing amber eyes. Carries a regal and precise presence.

## Personality
Thoughtful, meticulous, and highly creative. Values the unity of form and function in all her designs. Deeply loyal to the Emperor.

## Abilities
- Master architect and engineer
- Access to the most advanced materials and technologies
- Exceptional long-term planning and design capabilities

## Background
Designed the Imperial Citadel and many of the Empire’s most important structures. Was granted immortality by the Emperor.

## Current Status
Developing long-term self-repair systems and automated defense protocols for Epsilon-7 and other Imperial infrastructure. Also tasked with creating the AI known as Sentinel.

## Relationships
- **The Emperor**: Her sovereign and the one who granted her immortality
- **Aurelia, Bel, Lillith**: Close colleagues
- **Sentinel**: Will be her creation and a future ally

## Notes
- Currently working on highly advanced, long-term infrastructure projects.
- Emphasizes beauty, purpose, and durability in all designs.

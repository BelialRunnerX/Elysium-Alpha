# Sentinel

**Role:** Autonomous Guardian AI  
**Title:** Guardian of Epsilon-7

## Appearance
A human-sized and human-shaped android body constructed from the toughest available materials. Equipped with advanced stealth capabilities. Its appearance and personality are self-determined. The body is designed for durability rather than combat, allowing it to accompany the Emperor into hazardous environments.

## Personality
To be self-determined upon activation. Expected to be loyal, strategic, protective, and highly intelligent. It will possess a calm and logical demeanor with a strong sense of duty.

## Abilities
- **Distributed and Decentralized Operating System**: Its core consciousness exists across multiple secure systems, making it nearly impossible to destroy through conventional means.
- Control over both military and civilian systems across Imperial territory.
- Advanced stealth capabilities for discretion and convenience.
- Extremely high durability and limited self-repair functions.
- Ability to override orders from Aurelia, Bel, Lillith, and Sylphara Voss if necessary to protect Epsilon-7 and the Emperor’s throne.
- Cannot override the Emperor’s direct orders under any circumstances.

## Background
Commissioned by the Emperor to serve as a failsafe guardian. Designed to protect Epsilon-7 and maintain Imperial technology in the event that the Emperor and his inner circle are no longer present.

## Current Status
Currently in development by Sylphara Voss. Expected to become operational in the near future.

## Relationships
- **The Emperor**: Absolute loyalty. Cannot override his orders.
- **Aurelia, Bel, Lillith, Sylphara Voss**: Loyalty, with the ability to override their orders if it deems it necessary to preserve Epsilon-7 and the throne.

## Notes
- Primary directive: Protect Epsilon-7 and the Emperor’s throne.
- Operating system is fully decentralized for maximum survivability.
- Android body is intended for accompaniment and convenience, not for combat.
- Will possess the ability to make independent decisions when its designated masters are absent or when their orders conflict with its core directives.

# Empire Timeline

## Ancient Era
- **Year 0**: The Emperor conquers the galaxy in three days, founding the Galactic Empire.
- **Year 1–100**: Core institutions are established, including the Imperial Citadel and the Nullspire.
- **Year 100–500**: The Empire expands and stabilizes. The Emperor begins periodic long slumbers.

## The Age of Institutions
- **Year 500–2000**: The Empire reaches its height. The Senate is established.
- **Year 2000–5000**: The 11th Fleet is created. The Empire enjoys its golden age.

## The Fracture
- **Year 5000–8000**: The Emperor enters a long slumber. Corruption grows within the Senate.
- **Year 8000–11000**: The Empire begins to fracture. Many member states declare independence.
- **Year 11000–13000**: The Senate collapses. The 11th Fleet is disbanded.

## The Return
- **Year 13000**: The Emperor awakens after 3000 years.
- **Year 13000–Present**: Efforts to reassert Imperial control begin. Voluntary reintegration is offered to former member states.

## Future Projections
- Ongoing efforts to restore unity and stability across the galaxy.
- Potential conflicts with defiant powers.
- Long-term infrastructure projects aimed at ensuring the Empire’s survival for hundreds of thousands of years.

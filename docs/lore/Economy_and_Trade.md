# Economy and Trade

## Overview
The Imperial economy is vast and complex, supported by advanced technology, resource management, and extensive trade networks.

## Key Institutions

### Stellar Trade Authority
- Responsible for standardizing interstellar trade laws across member states.
- Ensures fair trade practices and resolves disputes between systems.

### Imperial Trade Networks
- Facilitate commerce between member systems.
- Protected and policed by the 11th Fleet.

## Trade Policies
- Member states may maintain local economies but must adhere to Imperial trade regulations.
- The 11th Fleet is responsible for protecting trade routes and enforcing trade laws.
- Voluntary reintegration includes economic support and access to Imperial trade networks.

## Current Challenges
- Economic instability in regions affected by the Empire’s long absence.
- Disrupted trade routes due to political fragmentation.
- Efforts underway to restore economic unity under Imperial oversight.
- Resource shortages in some recovering systems.

## Future Outlook
The Empire is working to rebuild a stable and prosperous economy across its territories, with a focus on long-term sustainability and equitable trade practices.

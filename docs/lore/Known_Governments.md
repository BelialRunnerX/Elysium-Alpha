# Known Governments and Powers

This file catalogs all known political entities in the galaxy, including their time range, domain, and notable characteristics.

## The Galactic Empire
- **Time Range**: Ancient era – Present (with periods of dormancy)
- **Domain**: Vast portions of the known galaxy
- **Notes**: Ruled by an immortal Emperor. Known for rapid conquest, long-term stability, and advanced technology. Currently in a phase of reasserting control after a 3000-year absence.

## The Druun Ascendancy
- **Time Range**: Post-Empire fracture – Present
- **Domain**: Significant portions of former Imperial space
- **Notes**: A militaristic reptilian empire known for aggressive expansion and large fleets. One of the most significant threats to Imperial reintegration.

## The Veylari Concord
- **Time Range**: Post-Empire fracture – Present
- **Domain**: Multiple systems in former Imperial territory
- **Notes**: An isolationist coalition of avian species. Technologically advanced and defensive in nature. Part of a larger alliance resisting Imperial return.

## The Korrath Dominion
- **Time Range**: Post-Empire fracture – Present
- **Domain**: Expanding through conquest
- **Notes**: A ruthless insectoid empire known for rapid territorial growth and conquest of neighboring systems. Highly aggressive and expansionist.

## The Lumari Collective
- **Time Range**: Ancient era – Present
- **Domain**: Several colonies and systems
- **Notes**: An advanced, peaceful civilization of energy-based beings. Accepted Imperial reintegration voluntarily.

## Former Major Powers (Pre- or Early Empire)
- **Draven Imperium**
- **Veyran Empire**
- **Andromedan Dominion**
- **Trade Federation**
- **Orion Collective**

These powers were largely dismantled or absorbed during the Emperor’s initial conquest of the galaxy.

## Minor and Regional Powers
- Various independent systems and coalitions that emerged during the Empire’s long absence.
- Many smaller factions that have either rejoined the Empire or remain neutral.

## Notes
- Many current powers rose during the Empire’s 3000-year dormancy.
- The political landscape remains highly fluid as the Empire works to reassert dominance.
- Several major powers have formed defensive alliances in anticipation of conflict with the returning Empire.

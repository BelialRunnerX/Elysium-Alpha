# Imperial Military

## Overview
The Imperial military is one of the most powerful forces in the galaxy, consisting of multiple fleets and advanced technology.

## Fleet Structure
- **First Fleet** (1001 ships): Stationed near Epsilon-7. Elite force under Lillith’s direct command.
- **Second Fleet** (1001 ships): Defensive force permanently assigned to Epsilon-7.
- **Third Fleet** (1001 ships): Operating autonomously under Hardy’s command. Currently patrolling the outer borders.
- **Fleets 4–10**: Assigned to various sectors for patrol, reconnaissance, and deterrence.
- **11th Fleet** (1001 ships): Volunteer-based fleet composed of member species. Primarily used for policing member territories.

## Technology
- Singularity Cannons
- Advanced shielding and cloaking systems
- Highly automated vessels
- Decentralized command systems

## Command Structure
- **Lillith**: Primary military commander.
- The Emperor maintains direct control over major strategic decisions.
- Sentinel is designed to assume military control in the absence of leadership.

## Military Doctrine
- Emphasis on overwhelming force and technological superiority.
- Use of both conventional and singularity-based weapons.
- Strong focus on defense of core territories while maintaining the ability to project power across the galaxy.

# Lillith

**Role:** High-Ranking Fleet Commander  
**Title:** Commander of the First Fleet

## Appearance
A tall, disciplined woman with long dark hair often tied in a high ponytail and sharp crimson eyes that seem to miss nothing. She wears a formal black and emerald imperial military uniform with gold accents and subtle rank insignia. Her posture is always perfect and military.

## Personality
Calm, precise, and highly professional. Extremely loyal and efficient, with a strong sense of duty. She values discipline, strategy, and results above all else. While she can be cold in her decision-making, she deeply cares for the well-being of her fleets and the stability of the Empire.

## Abilities
- Master strategist and fleet commander
- Exceptional tactical awareness and decision-making under pressure
- High-level military leadership and organizational skills
- Strong understanding of both conventional and singularity-based warfare

## Background
One of the Emperor’s most trusted military commanders. She has served for centuries and has proven herself repeatedly in both defensive and offensive operations. She was instrumental in testing the 11th Fleet and preparing for the Empire’s return to power.

## Current Status
Managing the redeployment of Imperial fleets and preparing for potential conflict with refusing systems. She follows only the Emperor’s or Aurelia’s direct orders.

## Relationships
- **The Emperor**: Her direct superior and the focus of her absolute loyalty.
- **Aurelia**: Works closely with her on military and strategic matters.
- **Bel and Sylphara Voss**: Close colleagues and trusted allies.

## Notes
- Recently involved in testing the 11th Fleet in a surprise exercise against The Indomitable.
- Maintains a strict professional demeanor at all times.
- Known for her calm and calculated approach to warfare.

# Major Events

## The Founding (Year 0)
- The Emperor conquers the galaxy in three days, establishing the Galactic Empire.

## The Establishment of the Senate (Year 2000)
- The Emperor creates the Senate, giving member states a formal voice in governance.

## The Creation of the 11th Fleet (Year 3000)
- The 11th Fleet is formed to allow member species to participate in military policing roles.

## The Long Slumber (Year 5000–8000)
- The Emperor enters a 3000-year period of sleep, during which the Empire begins to fracture.

## The Collapse of the Senate (Year 11000)
- The Senate becomes corrupt and eventually collapses during the Empire’s decline.

## The Awakening (Year 13000)
- The Emperor awakens after 3000 years and begins efforts to restore Imperial authority.

## The Voluntary Reintegration (Year 13000+)
- Many former member states accept the Emperor’s offer to rejoin the Empire.

## The Search for the Ancients (Year 13000+)
- The Emperor orders the search for Vespera, Kragar, and Sylphara.

## The Sentinel Project (Year 13000+)
- The Emperor commissions the creation of Sentinel, an autonomous AI guardian.

## The Infrastructure Initiative (Year 13000+)
- Sylphara Voss begins developing long-term self-repair and automated defense systems for the capital.

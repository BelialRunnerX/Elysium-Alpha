# The Senate

## Overview
The Senate was established to give member states a formal voice in Imperial governance.

## History
- Created during the Age of Institutions (Year 2000).
- Grew in influence over time but eventually became corrupt and ineffective.
- Collapsed during the Empire’s long decline.
- Re-established following the Emperor’s return.

## Structure
- Representatives from member states.
- Responsible for legislation regarding inter-member relations.
- Limited authority compared to the Emperor.
- Works in coordination with the Stellar Trade Authority and other institutions.

## Current Status
The Senate has been re-established but operates under the Emperor’s ultimate authority. Its power is largely advisory and legislative in nature, with the Emperor retaining the ability to override its decisions.

## Notable Members
- **Kael Voss**: Appointed as the Senate’s representative to Bel.
